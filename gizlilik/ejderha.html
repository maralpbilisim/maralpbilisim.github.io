Ejderhayı Besle 3D — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Information we collect

The app has no accounts and does not ask for your name, e-mail, phone number or location. Your progress and settings are stored only on your device.

World ranking

When you use the world ranking, your in-game name, your best score and an anonymous ID created by Google Firebase are sent to Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) servers and shown to other players in the ranking. You do not have to use your real name; use a nickname. You can change the name in the game at any time. To request deletion, write to maralpbilisim@gmail.com.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure performance and prevent fraud, AdMob may collect your advertising ID, device/app information, IP address (approximate location) and your ad interactions. This is processed by Google; we have no access to it. More: Google Privacy Policy.

Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland see Google's consent form for personalized ads; you can change your choice in the app settings. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID under Settings > Google > Ads.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child's information was collected, write to maralpbilisim@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date. Developer: Maral Apps — maralpbilisim@gmail.com
